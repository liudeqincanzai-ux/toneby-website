Frequently Asked Questions

Are my photos uploaded to a server?
No. Every photo is processed 100% locally on your device. Toneby has no accounts, no cloud uploads and no tracking — your images never leave your phone.
Can I import my own .cube LUT files?
Yes. Import standard .cube LUT files and use them in the LUT camera and the reference-color workspace, alongside the built-in film-style collection.
What is included in Pro?
Pro unlocks the full advanced toolkit — including premium LUT packs and pro grading tools. One-time purchase through Google Play billing.
Which devices are supported?
Toneby runs on Android 10 and above and is optimized for the latest Android versions, including full support for edge-to-edge and dark mode.
