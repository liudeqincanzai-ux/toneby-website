中文

1. 简介

Toneby（以下简称"本应用"）是一款本地照片调色工具。我们非常重视您的隐私，本政策说明本应用如何处理您的信息。

2. 我们收集哪些信息

本应用的设计目标是所有照片均在您的设备本地处理：照片不会被上传到任何服务器，我们不会收集、存储或分享您的照片内容。

本应用不使用第三方广告 SDK，不收集设备标识符，不收集位置信息，不收集使用统计数据。

3. 权限说明

相机权限：仅用于在应用内拍照取景，照片保存在您的设备上。

4. 应用内购买

应用内购买通过 Google Play 处理。付款信息由 Google 负责，本应用无法访问您的支付账号信息。

5. 数据分享

我们不会向任何第三方出售、出租或分享您的个人信息。

6. 您的权利

由于我们不收集任何个人信息，您无需申请删除数据。如需联系我们，请发送邮件至：liudeqincanzai@gmail.com

7. 政策更新

本政策如有更新，将在本页面公布。

English

1. Introduction

Toneby ("the App") is a local photo color grading tool. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

2. What Data We Collect

The App is designed to process all photos entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to any server. We do not collect, store, or share your photo content.

We do not use third-party advertising SDKs. We do not collect device identifiers, location data, or usage analytics.

3. Permissions

Camera: Used only for capturing photos within the App. Photos are saved locally on your device.

4. In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed through Google Play. Payment information is handled by Google and is not accessible to the App.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party.

6. Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no data to delete. If you have any questions, please contact us at: liudeqincanzai@gmail.com

7. Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page.
